# Widget Assembly Manual

## Overview

The assembly proceeds in four stages. Each stage depends on the one before it, so do not skip ahead. Read this section end to end before you pick up a single tool.

## Required Parts

- Frame plate, anodized
- Hex bolts, eight of them
  - Washers ship in the bolt bag
- Torque wrench, calibrated this year

## Procedure

1. Seat the frame plate flat on the jig.
2. Thread every bolt by hand before torquing any of them.
3. Torque to spec in a star pattern, never in a circle.

### Key Points:

A hand-thread that binds means the plate is not seated. Back it out and start the stage again rather than forcing the bolt.

## Verification

Verify the assembly cold, before the first run. A warm frame hides the very gap you are looking for.

a. Check the gap with a feeler gauge at all four corners.
b. Re-torque after ten minutes of rest.
c. Record the final reading in the build log.

## Troubleshooting

### Key Points:

A reading that drifts between the first and second torque pass is a seating problem, not a calibration problem.
